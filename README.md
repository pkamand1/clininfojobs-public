## About ClinInfoJobs

ClinInfoJobs is a job board for people who want to build a career in clinical and health informatics but do not hold, and do not intend to pursue, an RN, NP, PA, MD, DO, PharmD or comparable clinical license. Much of the informatics hiring market is written as though a nursing background were the only way in, and graduates in biology, computer science, biomedical engineering, statistics, public health, nutrition and the laboratory sciences are left to sift through hundreds of postings to discover which ones are genuinely open to them. ClinInfoJobs does that sifting for them.

The board is written for new graduates at the certificate, bachelor's and master's level and for career changers arriving from science, engineering, data and public health, although it carries mid-level and senior roles as well.

## What the board covers

The scope is health informatics in its broad sense: clinical informatics, health and healthcare data science, biomedical data science, consumer health informatics, public health informatics and public health data science, and healthcare biostatistics where a statistics degree is not required. Postdoctoral positions, fellowships, internships, co-ops and apprenticeships in these fields appear alongside permanent roles.

Listings come from hospitals and health systems, payers, health IT and digital health companies, pharmaceutical companies and CROs, public health agencies, universities and nonprofits across North America, Latin America and the Caribbean, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Asia Pacific.

As of November 2026 the board carries close to 1,000 open listings from more than 350 employers, and it reads postings in more than ten languages.

## The principles every listing meets

1. No clinical license required. A posting that requires a clinical license or professional registration does not appear. Where a license is only preferred, the listing appears and says so.
2. Straight to the employer. Every Apply link opens the employer's own careers site or official program page. The board never links to recruiters, staffing agencies, aggregators or other job boards.
3. Informatics, not adjacent work. Medical coding, general administration and IT support roles are kept off the board, however often they carry an informatics label.
4. Honest tagging. Every listing is tagged for degree level, licensure, Epic or Oracle Health certification and work arrangement, and pay is shown only when the employer has posted it.
5. Current listings only. Postings that have closed, passed their deadline or reached thirty days of age are taken down, and links are rechecked continuously.

## For job seekers

The board can be filtered by track, region, country, level, work arrangement and posting date, and it offers dedicated pages for early-career programs, remote work by country, roles suited to particular degrees, posted pay by track, the skills employers name most often, and a glossary of informatics terminology. Readers can subscribe to a Monday email, save a search so that the email favors their region, track or employer, or follow any view by RSS. A community is gathering on Discord and Reddit as well.

## For employers

Employers can post a role directly at [clininfojobs.com/post](https://clininfojobs.com/post), and an employer's first listing is free. Audience figures are published at [clininfojobs.com/employers](https://clininfojobs.com/employers), and a monthly report on informatics hiring drawn from the board's own data is published at [clininfojobs.com/hiring](https://clininfojobs.com/hiring).

## About this repository

This is a public overview of ClinInfoJobs. The software that runs the board, its list of employer sources, its screening rules and its listing data are maintained privately and are not published here. Questions about partnerships, sponsorship, data or press are welcome by email.
